Eight true stories of countries becoming friends.

Why We Made This Website

Most of the news about countries is about them arguing — borders, wars, trade fights. But history is also full of moments where rival nations did the opposite, and those stories rarely get told. Terminal Earth collects eight of them, all true. We built it like an airport because every one of these stories starts the same way: something crossing a border. Pick up a passport below and see where it goes.
